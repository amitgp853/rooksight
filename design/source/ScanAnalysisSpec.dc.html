Eval bar: two layouts explored

A · Horizontal, above the board Board 390 px (48.75 px squares)
B · Thin vertical bar beside it Board 368 px (46 px squares)
Recommendation Ship A. Keep B as a setting later if people ask. A keeps the board full width, matches Game Review, and has room for a readable, signed number (+0.4, M3). B costs 6% of board width on a 390 px phone, and its 18 px bar only fits a 9 px number without the sign.
